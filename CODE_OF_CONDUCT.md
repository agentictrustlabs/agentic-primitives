# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

The short version: be respectful, assume good faith, critique code and ideas rather than people,
and keep the space welcoming to developers of every background and experience level.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers via the GitHub organization contacts.
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and
interactions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for
behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
